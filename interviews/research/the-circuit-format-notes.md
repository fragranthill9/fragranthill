## 2. Format / structure

What is sourced:
- Bloomberg's own framing: field-based, "goes behind the scenes with founders", "intimate interviews", host visits subjects at home/office (e.g., Chesky at his SF home; Siebel Newsom at Marin home; Wojcicki in Palo Alto; Kalshi founders walking NYC streets; Stargate site walkthrough in Abilene with locals interviewed). Press release for S3: premiere online first, then split into two ~half-hour TV parts.
- Microsoft episode transcript fragments show Chang narrating on location ("on Microsoft's campus in Redmond"), three separate sit-downs sequenced Gates → Ballmer → Nadella, and a closing "quick rapid fire round" asking for short answers **[fragment]**.
- Dawn of the AI Wars: opens on the Bill Gates 1995 "Internet Tidal Wave" memo as the framing device (Chang's LinkedIn post), then Nadella in Redmond, then Altman; clip cut-downs are 2–3 min single-topic segments (tidal wave; how Altman uses ChatGPT).
- Stargate episode (per local coverage/podcast notes): facility walkthrough, Altman on AI's future, Crusoe founding story, local Abilene perspectives, energy/tariff risks — i.e., a reported mini-doc wrapping the CEO sit-down.

- 0:00–1:30 Cold open: 1995 Gates memo / "is AI the next tidal wave?"; quick tease sound-bites from Nadella and Altman; title card.
