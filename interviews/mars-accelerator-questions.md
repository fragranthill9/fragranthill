# Interview questions: 火星加速器 co-founder (ranked 1–60)

Ranking logic: Western-audience interest × her ability and willingness to answer well × clip value. Each item has the English question, a Chinese version you can say, and what it is for.

### Tier 1: must ask (1–15)

1. **The ground truth.** "You see hundreds of founders a year. If a Western investor only watched Unitree's IPO and the Beijing half-marathon, what is the single biggest thing they are getting wrong about China's embodied AI?"
   你们一年见几百个创始人。如果一个西方投资人只看到宇树上市和北京半马，他对中国具身智能最大的误解是什么？
   Why: lets her correct the West. Best possible opening clip.

2. **Your report's thesis.** "Your August report with 云启 and SEE Fund says the bottleneck is usable data, not data volume. What does 'usable' mean in practice, and who in China is actually producing it?"
   你们 8 月和云启、SEE Fund 的报告说瓶颈是可用数据而不是数据量。'可用' 到底指什么？国内谁在真正生产这种数据？
   Why: her own work; she has rehearsed answers; shows you did homework.

3. **The 2026 yardstick.** "陈亮 said at CES that 2026 is the year manipulation robots commercialise. It is late October. Name one deployment in your community that is running at a hundred units, unsubsidised, with a customer paying."
   陈亮在 CES 说 2026 是操作型机器人商业化元年。现在是 10 月底，你们社区里有没有一个案例是 100 台规模、客户真金白银付费、没有补贴的？
   Why: the Emily Chang dishwasher question, China version. Follow up: "If not yet, what is the date?"

4. **Where the 90+ data centres go.** "China has over 90 embodied data collection centres and the state wants 10,000 humanoids in commercial use by year end. IDC says 69% of shipments still go to research, demos and data centres. Is that a bridge to a real market or a market on its own?"
   国内有 90 多个数据采集中心，IDC 说 69% 的出货去了科研、展示和数采中心。这是通向真实市场的桥，还是本身就成了市场？
   Why: the Economist's exact question ("is the data effort worth it"); she can answer from the inside.

5. **Who gets a ticket.** "Chinese media call it '300 runners, 20 tickets'. H1 funding was tens of billions of yuan and the top 20 took most of it. What do you tell a founder who is number 150?"
   行业说 '300 家抢跑，20 张船票'。你会跟排在第 150 名的创始人说什么？
   Why: bubble question framed as advice; she will answer honestly.

6. **Her founder filter.** "Tell me about the last team you turned down, and the one you accepted the same week. What was the difference?"
   讲讲最近一个你们拒绝的团队，和同一周接受的团队。区别在哪？
   Why: story, not criteria. Terry Gross method.

7. **Fundraising playbook is dead.** "陈亮 says the fundraising playbook from the last cycle has 'basically failed' in embodied AI. What replaced it? Is it corporates and local governments instead of VCs?"
   陈亮说上个周期的融资套路在具身赛道基本失效了。那现在的新路径是什么？是产业龙头和地方政府替代了 VC 吗？
   Why: Western VCs want to understand China's capital structure: guidance funds, SOE capital, CVCs.

8. **Legs or wheels.** "Factories in your network: are they buying bipeds or wheeled bases? One founder said bipeds spend 40% of energy and 60% of compute on walking. Is the humanoid form a product decision or a fundraising decision?"
   你们社区里的工厂客户，买的是双足还是轮式底盘？人形是产品决策还是融资决策？
   Why: the sharpest insider debate in China this year; the West rarely hears it.

9. **The Unitree effect.** "Unitree listed at 600% up, fell 55%, and has rebounded. From where you sit, what did the IPO change for early-stage founders: valuations, exits, or the kind of people who now want to start robot companies?"
   宇树上市对早期创始人改变了什么：估值、退出预期，还是现在想做机器人的人变了？
   Why: the only China robot story every Western viewer already knows. Use it as a door, not the destination.

10. **ChatGPT moment: when.** "王兴兴 says 2 to 3 years, AgiBot says a GPT-3.5 moment in 3 to 5. What do you tell your founders to plan for, and what should they build in the meantime?"
   王兴兴说 2 到 3 年，智元说 3 到 5 年。你让创始人按什么时间表规划？在那之前该做什么？
   Why: timing question, Chang style. Her answer reveals how she invests.

11. **Supply chain as the moat.** "Jensen Huang said the US robotics industry 'relies deeply' on China's motors, magnets and rare earths. How much of your founders' cost advantage is supply chain and how much is engineering speed?"
   黄仁勋说美国机器人产业深度依赖中国的电机、磁材和稀土。你们创始人的成本优势里，供应链占多少，工程迭代速度占多少？
   Why: a question the West is obsessed with; she can answer without politics.

12. **Still imported.** "Which parts still come from abroad: planetary roller screws, tactile sensors, Nvidia Jetson? Where is the weakest link in the Chinese chain today?"
   哪些东西还得进口？行星滚柱丝杠、触觉传感器、英伟达 Jetson？今天中国链条上最弱的一环在哪？
   Why: shows you know the chain. You can add your own sensor-side view.

13. **The FCC and going global.** "In July the FCC put foreign humanoids on its covered list. Your programme added an overseas module. Where are your founders actually going: Europe, the Middle East, Southeast Asia? What does the US ban change?"
   7 月美国 FCC 把外国人形机器人列入清单。你们加了出海模块，创始人实际去哪：欧洲、中东、东南亚？美国的限制改变了什么？
   Why: Western audience, European angle, Munich summit next week.

14. **Her job.** "What does a day look like for you, versus 陈亮? And what did you leave to do this?"
   你和陈亮每天分别在做什么？你是放弃了什么来做这件事的？
   Why: nobody has written her story. Put it here, not at the start, so she is warm.

15. **What founders get wrong.** "After 174 companies through your camps, what is the mistake you see in the first meeting that predicts failure 18 months later?"
   174 家公司走过你们的营，第一次见面时哪种错误最能预测 18 个月后的失败？
   Why: evergreen wisdom clip; Andy Grove territory.

### Tier 2: strong (16–35)

16. "Who is the customer with budget in 2026: auto plants, logistics, pharmacies, hotels, or local governments?" 2026 年真正有预算的客户是谁？
17. "UBTech says 96% of its full-size humanoid revenue is industrial, Airbus took 100 units. Is aviation and auto the beachhead for everyone, or just for them?" 航空和汽车是所有人的滩头阵地，还是只是优必选的？
18. "What price does a humanoid or a manipulation arm need to hit before a factory manager buys without a subsidy?" 价格到多少，工厂老板会不靠补贴自己买？
19. "The price war: a biped under RMB 10,000 exists. Do cheap bodies help the ecosystem by getting hardware into labs, or do they poison the market with toys?" 万元以下的双足是在帮生态还是在毒化市场？
20. "What is a 具身智能 company's real gross margin today, and who is the first to turn an operating profit?" 今天真实的毛利是多少？谁最先实现经营利润？
21. "Stanford's AI Index found 89% success in simulation versus 12% in real homes. How do your founders close that gap, sim, teleoperation, or crowdsourced data?" 仿真 89% 成功率，真实家庭 12%。你们的创始人怎么补这个差距？
22. "World models: your report calls them data amplifiers, not substitutes. Where did you see a world model actually save a founder six months?" 世界模型什么时候真的帮一个创始人省了半年？
23. "Should a Chinese startup train its own VLA model or ride on open-source bases like GR00T and Qwen? Where is the moat if the model is open?" 自己训 VLA 还是用开源底座？模型开源了护城河在哪？
24. "Chips: how many of your founders are on Nvidia Jetson and how many on 地平线 or other domestic SoCs? Is domestic compute a choice or a hedge?" 英伟达还是地平线？国产算力是选择还是对冲？
25. "Hangzhou passed China's first local embodied-AI regulation in May. What does a city do that actually helps a founder, beyond money?" 除了钱，一个城市能为创始人做什么真正有用的事？
26. "Local government funds want local jobs and local factories. How do you coach founders to take that money without losing the company's direction?" 怎么拿地方政府的钱又不被带偏方向？
27. "Secondary shares of a star project reportedly went at a 50% discount with no takers. Have you seen valuations reset in the primary market, and by how much?" 一级市场估值重置了吗？幅度多大？
28. "If the funding squeeze everyone expects after November happens, which types of company die first and which type do you want to own?" 11 月后钱紧了，哪类公司先死？你想持有哪类？
29. "The IPO path: STAR Market, ChiNext, or Hong Kong 18C? Does the reported regulatory guidance asking for recurring revenue change how you coach founders?" 科创板、创业板还是港股 18C？监管要求有营收，会改变你的辅导方式吗？
30. "The talent question: half of the world's top AI researchers are of Chinese origin. Are the best embodied-AI researchers coming back from the US, and why?" 最好的具身研究者在回流吗？为什么？
31. "Your first cohort had founders from Japan and Indonesia. Can a non-Chinese founder succeed inside the Chinese ecosystem, and what would you tell a Swedish robotics founder who wants to build in Suzhou?" 一个瑞典机器人创始人想在苏州创业，你会怎么说？
32. "What is the role of 3D vision and tactile sensing in the next two years? Is perception solved, or is it the hidden bottleneck behind the model story?" 感知解决了吗，还是模型故事背后的隐形瓶颈？
33. "Tesla Optimus is ramping in Fremont. Do your founders watch Tesla, or do they watch each other?" 你们的创始人盯特斯拉还是盯彼此？
34. "Humanoid games, the half-marathon, dancing at the Spring Festival gala: are the spectacles helping or hurting serious founders?" 这些表演对认真做事的创始人是帮助还是伤害？
35. "What does the 15th Five-Year Plan's inclusion of embodied AI change on the ground: standards, procurement, training grounds?" 十五五把具身智能写进去，落地层面改变了什么？

### Tier 3: good if time (36–50)

36. "Which scene will surprise us: eldercare, retail, pharmacies, hotels, or something nobody is pitching yet?" 哪个场景会让我们意外？
37. "Home robots: 陈亮 said 10,000 units at RMB 10,000 is 'very hard'. What has to be true for a home robot to work in China before it works in the West?" 家用机器人在中国先跑通需要什么条件？
38. "Dexterous hands: 20-DoF hands and a RMB 800M hand industrial park. Is the hand the next battleground after the leg?" 灵巧手是腿之后的下一个战场吗？
39. "Data ownership: when a factory lets a robot collect data, who owns it, the customer, the robot maker, or the model company?" 数据归谁？
40. "Standards: MIIT set up a humanoid standards committee. Will Chinese standards travel abroad the way EV charging standards did?" 中国标准会像充电标准一样走出去吗？
41. "Your community includes six listed companies' robotics heads. What does a listed incumbent want from a 20-person startup?" 上市公司要从 20 人的创业公司得到什么？
42. "How do you measure your own success: companies funded, companies alive at year three, or exits?" 你怎么衡量自己的成功？
43. "Zhu Xiaohu exited humanoid investments in 2025 and said 'everyone can backflip, where is the commercialisation'. Was he early, wrong, or right?" 朱啸虎是早了、错了还是对了？
44. "What is the difference between founders from autonomous driving, from academia, and from hardware OEMs? Who is winning?" 自动驾驶、学术、硬件出身的创始人，谁在赢？
45. "Europe's AI Act and Machinery Regulation arrive in 2027. Is that a wall or a 'trusted corridor' for Chinese robots?" 欧盟法规是墙还是通道？
46. "What do you need from Europe that you cannot get in China: customers, certification, talent, or capital?" 你们从欧洲需要什么是国内拿不到的？
47. "The Munich Europe–China humanoid summit is next week. If you had five minutes with Bosch, what would you ask them?" 如果和博世有五分钟，你问什么？
48. "Robotics as the 'next EV': is the DJI and BYD playbook, vertical integration plus iteration speed, the right analogy, or is this industry different?" 大疆和比亚迪的打法适用吗？
49. "Safety: a 60-kilogram humanoid next to a worker. Who in China is doing the unglamorous safety and certification work?" 谁在做安全和认证这些不性感的工作？
50. "What is one Western robotics company you admire, and one you think is overrated?" 你欣赏的和高估的西方机器人公司各一个？

### Tier 4: personal, closers, and backups (51–60)

51. "What did you believe about this industry a year ago that you no longer believe?" 一年前相信、现在不信的一件事？
52. "The founder in your community you would bet your own money on, and why." 你会押自己钱的创始人是谁？
53. "What do Chinese founders ask you about the West that surprises you?" 中国创始人问你关于西方的什么问题让你意外？
54. "If you could change one policy tomorrow, what would it be?" 明天能改一条政策，改哪条？
55. "What will the community look like in 2028: how many founders, how many countries?" 2028 年火星社区是什么样？
56. "What is a Chinese idiom or phrase that explains this industry's year better than any English word?" 哪个中文词最能解释这一年？
57. "Ask me: what do you want to know about how Europe sees China's robots?" 反过来问我：你想知道欧洲怎么看？
58. "Who should I interview next, and what should I ask them?" 下一个该采访谁，问什么？
59. "What would make a Western investor or buyer welcome at your next demo day?" 怎样的西方投资人或买家会在你们 demo day 受欢迎？
60. "Finish this sentence: in 2027, Chinese embodied AI will be judged on ___." 2027 年，中国具身智能会被用什么来评判？
