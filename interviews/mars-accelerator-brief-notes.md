# Research notes: 火星加速器 (Mars Accelerator) — raw facts collected 2026-10-27

## Confirmed via search snippets (Chinese sites block direct fetch from this environment)
- Founder: 陈亮 (Chen Liang). Left 科沃斯 X-MAN accelerator on 2024-11-05 after 7.5 years; ran AI/robotics industry ecosystem there. Launched 火星加速器 late 2024.
- Positioning: 具身智能、机器人全产业链创业服务 + 早期投资; founding team "8 years in robotics, accompanied 400+ founders"; stated aim "有全球影响力的具身智能创新生态社区".
- Thesis (四木专访, 2025-04): robotics will be the fastest-growing industry of the next 20-30 years; 具身智能 is a "capital-siphoning" track; wants an independent, third-party founder community rather than a corporate accelerator.
- Thesis (产业营开营, 2025-04): robots have no standard form factor like phones or cars; demand side is fragmented; therefore the community acts as "连接器 / 路由器" between tech supply, scenarios and capital.
- Thesis (36氪 / 中国创投网, 2025-11 "具身智能创始人，来找我面试了"): old-cycle fundraising playbook no longer works in embodied AI; early founders prefer to partner with 产业链龙头; of 500+ embodied-AI companies served, 40+ closed a new round in 2025, expects more in 2026; two 产业营 + two 孵化营 run, 174 companies served, third 产业营 recruiting.
- CES 2026 (Jan 2026, 钛媒体): 陈亮 moderated a roundtable (灵御智能 金戈, 途灵科技 杨章欣, HTC VIVE Jase Qiang, 联想创投 史晨星); consensus: 2026 is "操作型机器人商业化落地元年". Described as running China's largest embodied-robot founder community, 500+ founders.
- Programs: 火星具身智能产业营 第一期 2025-03-27..30 Suzhou 相城环秀湖, ~30 founders; module 2 in Shenzhen. 火星·具身智能创始人大会暨首届火星具身智能创业大赛, Shenzhen 2025-04-25, co-hosted with FAIR plus and 深圳市机器人协会; theme "从乱战到共链，生态即未来"; released 具身智能产业白皮书, open-source 具身智能测评数据集, 具身智能人才白皮书; 4-month full-scholarship accelerator (Jun 28 - Sep 28 2025); targets pre-A teams across chips, base software, components, vertical models, bodies, solutions. Suzhou 具身智能极客营 / 赢在苏州 specialty contest June 2025: 75 global teams, 20+ to finals, up to RMB 30M landing support; sponsored by Suzhou science bureau, organised by 元禾控股.
- Market context 2026: H1 2026 embodied-AI funding in China > RMB 90B (IT桔子), ~5x YoY, 300+ deals; top 20 companies took ~60%; investor logic shifting "从后空翻到搬砖头"; 2027 seen as the make-or-break mass-production year.
