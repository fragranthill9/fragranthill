1. Editorial content on robotics, semiconductors, automotive, emerging tech, investment (video, LinkedIn, newsletter, YouTube, Instagram, WeChat). Assets I hold: a channel to host celebrity concerts; a completed Lang Lang interview; deep automotive and sensor industry background; a Swedish company; an operating partner in China. I need the partner's network in automotive, robotics and other technical industries for sponsorship.

### The task
Produce an executive-level project proposal for a partner (do not name the partner). Bird's-eye view only. No detailed implementation plan, no dates, no timelines, no revenue forecasts.

Exactly three one-page documents, each in English and in Chinese (six files), every block visualised:
1. System architecture: the five pillars, how they relate, in which sequence they activate, and how each one is monetised.
2. Monetisation: who pays, through which revenue stream, into which pillar; when each pillar pays (cash now, recurring, equity).
3. The project, "Encore" under pillar 4: sponsorship and fundraising for Lang Lang's piano tour next year, sold to automotive, robotics and technology brands. I get paid when a sponsorship closes. Payback is meant to be within about two months (deposit on signature). This is the first and most visible monetisation project and should repeat as a product line.

### Hard requirements
- Visual, not text. Diagrams, flows, charts, icons, tiles. Text only as labels and short captions. Do not write paragraphs. Do not deliver a "structure" of boxes with sentences in them.
- Google colour palette (blue #4285F4, red #EA4335, yellow #FBBC04, green #34A853, purple #A142F4) on a white background. Bright and light.
- Chinese version must be a full equal, not a translation afterthought. Fonts that render Chinese correctly (Noto Sans SC).
- No broken or cramped graphics. Test every figure at desktop and narrower widths. Nothing clipped.
- Every number must be a real, reported figure with its source. If a number is an estimate, mark it. Do not invent fee amounts or forecasts. Prices for Encore packages are "set per market, confirmed in first pitches".
- Each document is a separate file so one can be edited without touching the others. Also give a plain-text reading summary.
- Premium, investor-grade design.
