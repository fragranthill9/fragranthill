## Page 1 · System architecture — one core, five pillars, one network
- Five pillar cards: Editorial (the voice), Advisory (paid answers), Community (the vetted room), Live & Venues (the stage, product Encore), Acceleration (the pipeline). Each: what, products, who pays, and when it pays (pays later / cash now / recurring / cash now / equity).
- Shared core: editorial voice, relationship graph (one CRM), production line (record once, six channels, two languages), commercial desk and back office.
- Inputs already held: founder domain depth, stage and talent access, partner assets, two-market base.
- How the pillars feed each other: Live → Editorial (content) → Community (audience) → Advisory (clients) → Acceleration (deal flow) → Live (sponsors, founders).
- Why one system: one network, one production line, one voice.
- Activation sequence gated by conditions: Phase A cash (Live + Advisory), Phase B recurring (Editorial season + Community), Phase C equity (Acceleration).

## Page 2 · Monetisation — six revenue streams across five pillars
- Streams: sponsorship, service fees, membership, tickets and hospitality, licences and reports, equity and carry. Each with who pays, which pillars, and when it pays.
- Matrix pillar × stream (primary and secondary).
- How each pillar makes money, with one benchmark each (The Circuit, SemiAnalysis, vetted founder clubs, Semafor, a16z/YC).
- When each stream pays: cash now (Live, Advisory), recurring (Editorial, Community), equity (Acceleration).
- Who does the work, bird's-eye: human, digital agents, partner.
- Facts: Semafor 50–60% event sponsorship; SemiAnalysis under 5% subscriptions; 吴晓波年终秀 90% sponsorship; European sponsorship €34.5bn.
