## Page 1 · System architecture (drawn as a layered map)
- Top row: five buyers (brands and sponsors, clients, members, brands and sponsors, founders and investors) with € arrows down into the pillar they pay.
- Middle: five pillar cards with icon, three products and a "when it pays" chip (pays later / cash now / recurring / cash now / equity).
- Below: the shared core (editorial voice, relationship graph, production line, commercial desk) and the inputs already held, with arrows up.
- Second row: the pillar cycle (Live → Editorial → Community → Advisory → Acceleration → Live) around one network, and the activation staircase A cash → B recurring → C equity with a gate under each step.

## Page 2 · Monetisation (drawn as a money flow)
- Flow diagram: four buyer types → six revenue streams → five pillars, bands coloured by pillar.
- Bar chart of where benchmarked media houses earn: Semafor events 55%, 吴晓波年终秀 sponsorship 90%, SemiAnalysis research 95%, Dedao courses 64%, The Information subscriptions 90%.
- Lanes for when each pillar pays (cash now: Live, Advisory; recurring: Editorial, Community; equity: Acceleration).
- Unit-of-sale tiles per pillar and a who-does-the-work strip (human, digital agents, partner).
