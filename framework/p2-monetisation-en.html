How each pillar makes money

1 · Editorial · Season sponsorshipOne presenting sponsor per season, every free distribution window. Licensing of episodes to platforms.Benchmark: The Circuit runs one presenting sponsor per season.
2 · Advisory · Retainers and reportsRetainers and projects priced on outcome; co-branded reports licensed per seat.Benchmark: SemiAnalysis earns over 95% from research and consulting.
3 · Community · Two membership tiersAn expensable base tier; a vetted club priced on access. Courses and trips sold per seat.Benchmark: vetted founder clubs sustain four- to five-figure annual dues.
4 · Live & Venues · Sponsorship packagesSold on hospitality first, association second, content third, reach last. Deposit on signature.Benchmark: Semafor earns 50–60% of revenue from sponsored convenings.
5 · Acceleration · Fees and carryProgramme fee per company, scout fee per round, carry through the partner's fund.Benchmark: a16z and YC run media as a deal-flow funnel.
