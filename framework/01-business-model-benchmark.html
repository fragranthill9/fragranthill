The money is never in the content. It is in the room the content opens.

We benchmarked twenty-four media businesses that turned a voice into profit: celebrity houses, creator channels, venture-capital media, premium journalism, data platforms, event companies and paid clubs, in the United States, Europe and China. The pattern is consistent. Free editorial earns trust; the profit sits in a high-ticket product next to it: a sponsored tentpole, a data product, a vetted room.

Six rules we take into the Fragrant Hill design

1. One ritual, not random content. Every durable model has a single repeatable thing a sponsor or platform pays to stand beside. Ours is the interview series and the annual stage.

2. Sell the room before the reach. Semafor, FT Live, Bloomberg Live and the Chinese year-end shows all monetise access. Sponsorship of a live property needs introductions, not followers.

3. Build a SemiAnalysis, not a Stratechery. In a B2B niche the newsletter is marketing. The profit is in data, reports and advisory priced at €20k and up.

4. Two-tier everything. The Information, Dedao and YPO all run a base tier and a 2–5× premium tier. Membership at €400 and a vetted club at €4–8k.

5. Media as deal flow is real, but it is last. a16z and YC absorb media costs in fund economics. We earn that right after the first four pillars pay for themselves.

6. Keep overhead near zero. Hello Sunshine, Goop and Chief all cut staff. Every.to runs $3M on 15–25 people with AI doing production. That is our operating model.
