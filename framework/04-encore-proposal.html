Encore · the first monetisation project

A presenting-partner programme for a world-class pianist's 2027 European tour.

Encore is Fragrant Hill's recurring live product: we package a celebrity concert tour as a sponsorship, hospitality and content property for automotive, robotics and technology brands moving between Europe and China. The first edition anchors on a pianist we have already interviewed and can host, with a Nordic and Western European leg in 2027. Sponsors pay 50% on signature, which is what puts first cash inside 60 days of launch.

Why this property, why these sponsors, why now

The property. A pianist with sold-out halls across Europe and a claimed 16–20 million social following, a current Deutsche Grammophon album, an active autumn 2026 European run and listed 2027 dates in London, Hamburg and Bonn. No whole-tour presenting partner has ever been announced for this artist. Single concerts have carried title sponsors from banks and a casino resort. A Nordic leg is open.

What we hold. A completed long-form interview with the artist, a channel to host celebrity concerts, and a Stockholm base next to halls of 1,300–1,800 seats in Stockholm, Gothenburg, Oslo and Copenhagen, with Hamburg at 2,100 and Paris at 2,400 one flight away.

The sponsors. European premium car makers have sponsored classical music for decades: one festival's main sponsor since 1995, an orchestra and its international tours since 2011, "Opera for All" since 1997, a Nordic symphony's principal sponsor in Gothenburg. The playbook is proven. Chinese automotive, robotics and consumer-tech brands entering Europe have not used it yet. Their European marketing has gone to football, where one EV maker's tournament deal lifted awareness from 28% to 46%.

The buyer's need. A brand launching in the Nordics needs a room where dealers, fleet buyers, journalists and officials want to be. A concert hall is that room. Hospitality, not logo placement, is where B2B sponsors measure return.

Why now. November is when 2027 marketing budgets are set. The artist's 2027 European dates are being booked now. The partner's network reaches the robotics and automotive decision-makers we need, and Fragrant Hill reaches the stage. Neither side can do this alone, and neither needs to fund the other: revenue is shared on closed sponsorships.

Why it repeats. Encore is a product line, not a one-off. The same package ladder sells an annual tour, four executive salons on the same stages, and a sponsor dinner per city. Every edition feeds the interview series, the member list and advisory pipeline.

Risks and how they are covered

Category conflict. The artist has a history of automotive relationships and a 2026 appearance with a Chinese car group. Before any car brand is pitched, we confirm in writing which categories are open. If automotive is closed, the presenting tier goes to robotics and consumer technology, where no conflict exists.

Sales cycle. Sixty days is below the market norm. We mitigate with warm introductions only, a 30-brand list ranked by budget timing, and a base case that needs three signatures, not ten. If by week 6 fewer than five term sheets are live, we extend the window and tell the partner.

Artist, venue and payment. Cancellation insurance on the artist's dates; venues held on option before the first pitch; deposits non-refundable except for cancellation by the tour; category exclusivity written into each contract so one sponsor's exit does not unwind the others.

The ask

From the partner: twenty warm introductions to marketing or brand decision-makers in automotive, robotics and consumer technology with 2027 European plans, within three weeks of go; one named sponsor-side champion; and a decision on revenue share for introduced sponsors.

From Fragrant Hill: the property, the packages, the pitch, contracts, production, hospitality and content; weekly pipeline reporting; and the next three Encore editions offered to the partner first.

Decision needed: go or no-go on the 60-day launch by 15 November 2026.
