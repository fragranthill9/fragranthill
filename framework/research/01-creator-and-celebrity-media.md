# Research notes 1 — Celebrity- and creator-founded media companies (collected 2026-11-03)

[R] = reported by a named outlet or company. [E] = third-party or analyst estimate.

## Hello Sunshine (Reese Witherspoon, 2016)
- Candle Media bought control at a $900M valuation, Aug 2021 [R, Variety]. Oct 2026: Witherspoon and Mediawan bought it back for "low- to mid-eight figures" [R-sourced, Axios], roughly a 90%+ write-down. ~50% of staff cut Oct 30 2026 [R, TheWrap].
- Reese's Book Club: 6.2M readers, ~$900M in US book sales driven [R, company claim].
- Lesson: the cheap curatorial ritual (monthly book pick) outlived the $900M production thesis.

## Service95 (Dua Lipa, 2021)
- Free weekly newsletter, podcast "At Your Service" (one season BBC-sponsored), book club, Spotify book-club podcast (2025). No paywall; monetised through platform partnerships (BBC, Spotify, Google Maps) and brand partnerships. No revenue or subscriber figures public. Small London team.
- Lesson: a voice-led free newsletter plus one monthly ritual is enough to become a platform partner.

## TeaTime Pictures (Dakota Johnson, Ro Donnelly, 2019)
- Boat Rocker minority equity stake Dec 2021 (undisclosed) [R]; Sony Pictures Television first-look deal July 2025 [R, Deadline]. Staff likely under 15 [E].
- Lesson: sell a first-look to a bigger house to cover overhead; keep the curatorial brand independent.

## The Circuit with Emily Chang (Bloomberg Originals, 2023)
- 20–30 min founder-access interviews; seasons 3, 4, 5 each carried AT&T Business as presenting sponsor [R, Bloomberg Media]. Distributed on Bloomberg TV/app, YouTube, Prime Video, Tubi, podcast feed.
- Lesson: one title sponsor per season, every free distribution window; the host's access is the product.

## Silicon Valley Girl (Marina Mogilko)
- ~9M combined subscribers across 3 channels [R, YouTube]. Slow Ventures paid $1.7M for 5% of creator earnings for 30 years (2021) [R, TechCrunch]. 2024 EBITDA ~$1.2M; 2025 guidance $5.4M creator revenue, $28M LinguaTrip GMV [R, single secondary source].
- Ads under 20% of income; courses and the LinguaTrip marketplace carry the margin.

## Media-to-commerce comps
- Goop: $433M last valuation; ~$51–53M revenue 2025 [E]; 18% staff cut 2024; not yet profitable. Newsletter was the funnel, beauty margin survived.
- Honest Company: FY2025 revenue $371.3M, net loss $15.7M [R, SEC]. Celebrity equity built CPG, not media.
- Archewell: ~$100M Netflix overall deal (2020) downgraded to first-look (2025). Output, not fame, renews deals.

## Cross-cutting takeaways
1. Every durable model pairs free editorial with one ritual (monthly pick, annual speech, weekly interview) that a sponsor or platform pays to stand beside.
2. Subscriptions are secondary everywhere except Dedao.
3. Exits that held value kept the audience asset (book club, app), not the production overhead.
4. Consumer products and venture-scale valuations are where celebrity media loses money.
