## How publishers use AI (2025–26)
- Axel Springer / Business Insider: AI use is "an expectation"; >70% staff on enterprise ChatGPT; "Business AI" byline for machine-drafted, human-edited stories.
- Semafor Signals: multilingual aggregation surfaces angles; journalists write.
- Bloomberg: AI bullet summaries since Jan 2025; ≥36 corrections by Mar 2025 (cautionary).
- Every: $2M ARR / $3M revenue (Feb 2026), ~15–25 people → ~$120–200k per head.
- Morning Brew: Notion agents handle 20% of inbound ops issues (vendor-reported).
- Digiday Q4 2025 (n=40): 93% of publishers use AI; 67% primarily for ops.
Pattern: transcription, clipping, translation, summaries, headlines, research digests and ops are automated; final editorial and sponsor relationships stay human.

## China–Europe specifics
- China platforms for B2B tech: 公众号 (lead capture to 企微), 视频号 (largest reach), Bilibili (trust, low ad income), 知乎 (authority), 小红书 (seeding), 抖音 (reach). Monetisation is lead and advisory conversion, not platform ad share.
- WeChat Official Account accepts foreign business licence for Service Accounts.
- ICP filing needs a Chinese entity or local sponsor (20–30 working days); host outside China otherwise.
- PIPL: domestic storage or one of three export mechanisms; first cross-border enforcement hit a European brand (Shanghai, May 2025).
- Invoicing: only a PRC-registered VAT payer issues fapiao; Chinese clients paying a Swedish AB must withhold ~6% VAT plus withholding tax. Lean houses invoice via a Chinese partner agency that remits net of its fee.
- Keep China accounts to industry and tech analysis; news/current-affairs services are restricted for foreign entities. Get local counsel.
