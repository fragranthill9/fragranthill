## Flywheels in one line each
- VC-as-media: content → founder mindshare → deal flow; cost absorbed by management fees.
- Newsletter-to-events (Semafor, Axios, Bloomberg, FT): free C-suite list → sponsored convenings; events 50–60% of Semafor revenue.
- Journalism-to-data (The Information, SemiAnalysis, PitchBook): articles build trust; data and consulting at 2–200× the article price carry the P&L.
- Community (Hampton, Chief, YPO, Zhenghedao): vetting and revenue thresholds justify $4k–17k/yr.

## What a small Stockholm robotics/semi/auto house should copy
1. SemiAnalysis structure: the newsletter is marketing; the business is €20–100k data products and advisory.
2. The Information's two-tier ladder: ~€400 individual → ~€1,000 Pro with databases.
3. Semafor's convening model: invite-only CEO/CTO list feeding 3–6 sponsored dinners a year.
4. YC's Requests for Startups as a lead magnet.
5. Sell pavilions to trade agencies, not booths to startups.
6. Hampton/Zhenghedao pricing: a vetted operator peer group at €4–8k/yr is defensible.
