## Market benchmarks
- Europe sponsorship market €34.45bn (2025, +4.7%); sport €24.79bn (72%); non-sport incl. arts and culture €9.66bn (28%) [R, ESA/Nielsen Sports 2026]. Germany €6.26bn, UK €6.15bn, Spain €2.18bn, Italy €2.01bn, France €1.85bn.
- ESA arts survey: 61% of brands do not sponsor culture, 62% want to [R].
- Global rights fees $97.5bn (2024) [R, IEG secondary].
- Activation ratio: 0.81:1 average, only 18% at ≥ 1:1 [R, WFA/Lumency]; rule of thumb 1:1 to 2:1.
- Agency commission: 20% average, range 10–35%; retainer + commission standard [R, IEG].
- Sales cycle: 9–18 months for mid-six / low-seven-figure deals [R, vendor playbook]; sponsors want 3–6 months activation lead time.
- Payment terms: 50% on signature, 50% 30–60 days pre-event (contract samples) [R].
- Live Nation FY2025: Sponsorship & Advertising $1.33bn vs total $25.2bn (≈5–6%), AOI ≈64% margin; >70% of 2026 commitments pre-sold [R, 10-K].
- Hospitality is "where the real ROI lives" for B2B sponsors; one marketer measured 2× revenue growth among clients who attended [R, anecdotal].
