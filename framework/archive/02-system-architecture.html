One editorial core. Five product lines. One flywheel.

Fragrant Hill is built as a single operating system, not five businesses. Every pillar draws on the same four inputs we already hold, runs through the same core, and feeds the next pillar. Live brings the first cash, Editorial builds the audience, Advisory and Community turn trust into recurring revenue, and Acceleration is where the long-term value accrues.

Why one core

A single relationship graph is the real asset. A sponsor of a concert is a future advisory client, a guest on the interview show, and a potential limited partner. If each pillar keeps its own list, the flywheel never turns.

A single production unit cuts the cost that has hurt us so far: paying per recording for guests, crew and photography. One studio day is planned to feed the newsletter, two video channels, the Chinese channels and a sponsor deliverable.

Why this sequence

Live first. Sponsorship is sold on access and association, not on follower counts. It is the one revenue line we can close in weeks with the assets we already have.

Editorial second, with one theme. Six months of random content produced no audience. The fix is focus, not volume: one promise, one audience, one publishing rhythm.

Community and Advisory third. Both need trust that the first two pillars create. Acceleration last. Deal flow is a by-product of being the house everyone talks to.

What the partner brings

Data, reports and a founder network plug directly into Advisory (resale, co-branded research), Community (members, speakers) and Acceleration (deal flow, soft landing in Europe).

Industry access to sponsors in automotive and robotics is the missing input for Pillar 4. We hold the stage; the partner holds the introductions. Revenue is shared on closed sponsorships, so neither side funds the other.
