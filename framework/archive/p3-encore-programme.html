Encore · sponsorship programme for a world-class pianist's European tour

Sell the room, not the reach.

Encore packages a celebrity concert tour as a sponsorship, hospitality and content property for automotive, robotics and technology brands moving between Europe and China. Fragrant Hill holds the stage and the artist relationship. The partner holds the doors. Sponsors pay a deposit on signature, so the programme funds itself before the first show.

The offer in one block

Property
A European tour leg by a pianist who sells out the largest halls; Nordic cities plus one of Hamburg, Paris, London
Buyers
Brands launching or scaling in Europe: EV and premium automotive, robotics, semiconductors, consumer tech
Structure
1 presenting partner · 3 category-exclusive official partners · up to 6 hospitality partners
Payment
Deposit on signature, balance before the first show; standard event-sponsorship terms
Our revenue
Agency commission on rights fees (industry average 20%) plus production and hospitality fees
Repeats as
Annual tour · executive salons on the same stages · sponsor dinners per city

How the money flows

Package tiers · what each buys

Presenting partnerone · "presented by"
  • Naming on all tour materials, stage and screens in every city
  • Category exclusivity across the tour
  • Two VIP tables per city, artist meet-and-greet, one private recital or client evening
  • Twelve-month rights to tour film, interview cut-downs, behind-the-scenes series
  • Co-branded education activation with the artist's foundation; first refusal on the next edition
Rights fee: set per market against the precedents above and the tour budget; confirmed in the first three pitches
Official partnerthree · one per category
  • Category exclusivity: automotive · robotics and technology · finance or luxury
  • Programme and screen branding; one VIP table per city; backstage in two cities
  • Shared content pack and three bespoke clips
Hospitality partnerup to six
  • Premium seat blocks and lounge access in two cities; lounge branding; photo and social assets

Risks and controls

Category conflict. The artist has had automotive partners and appeared with a Chinese car group in 2026. Open categories are confirmed in writing before any brand is pitched. If automotive is closed, the presenting tier goes to robotics and consumer technology.
Sales cycle. Six-figure sponsorships normally take 9–18 months. We pitch only through warm introductions and start with a base of three signatures, not ten.
Artist, venue, payment. Cancellation insurance; venues held on option; deposits non-refundable except for cancellation by the tour; exclusivity written in so one exit does not unwind the others.

Why Encore is a product line, not a one-off

Annual tour editionSame package ladder, same sponsors offered first refusal. The artist roster can widen to other celebrity performers we can host.
Executive salonsFour salons a year on the same stages, 60–100 guests, two to three sponsors each. Feeds the Community pillar.
Sponsor dinnersOne client evening per city during the tour, produced for the sponsor; the hospitality line that B2B brands pay for.
Content and editorialEvery concert yields a tour film, artist interview material and a guest list; the Editorial pillar publishes it, the relationship graph keeps it.
