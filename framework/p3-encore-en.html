Encore · sponsorship programme for a world-class pianist's European tour

Sell the room, not the reach.

Encore packages a celebrity concert tour as a sponsorship, hospitality and content property for automotive, robotics and technology brands moving between Europe and China. Fragrant Hill holds the stage and the artist relationship. The partner holds the doors.

The offer in one block

Property
A European tour leg by a pianist who sells out the largest halls; Nordic cities plus one of Hamburg, Paris, London
Buyers
Brands launching or scaling in Europe: EV and premium automotive, robotics, semiconductors, consumer tech
Structure
1 presenting partner · 3 category-exclusive official partners · up to 6 hospitality partners
Payment
Deposit on signature, balance before the first show
Our revenue
Agency commission on rights fees (industry average 20%) plus production and hospitality fees
Repeats as
Annual tour · executive salons on the same stages · sponsor dinners

How the money flows

Package tiers

Presenting partnerone · "presented by"
  • Naming across the tour; category exclusivity
  • VIP tables per city; private recital for clients
  • 12-month content rights; first refusal on the next edition
Official partnerthree · one per category
  • Category exclusivity: automotive · robotics and tech · finance or luxury
  • Programme and screen branding; VIP table per city
  • Shared content pack
Hospitality partnerup to six
  • Premium seat blocks in two cities
  • Lounge access and branding; photo and social assets

Risks and controls

Category conflict. The artist has had automotive partners. Open categories are confirmed in writing before any brand is pitched; if automotive is closed, the presenting tier goes to robotics and consumer tech.
Sales cycle. Six-figure sponsorships normally take 9–18 months. We pitch only through warm introductions and start from a base of three signatures.
Artist, venue, payment. Cancellation insurance; venues on option; non-refundable deposits; exclusivity written in so one exit does not unwind the others.

Why it repeats

Annual tour editionSame package ladder; sponsors get first refusal. The roster can widen to other performers we can host.
Executive salonsSalons on the same stages, two to three sponsors each. Feeds the Community pillar.
Content and networkEvery concert yields a tour film, interview material and a guest list that the other pillars reuse.
