# Fragrant Hill · one studio day, six channels

Theme guardrail: every piece must connect to the house theme, **the people building machines, Europe ↔ China** (robotics, sensing, semiconductors, automotive, the investors behind them). If a piece does not, say so and propose how to angle it.

## Inputs
- Transcript (Descript or Riverside export) or notes; guest name, company, one-line why this guest matters; sponsor of the episode if any.

## Outputs, in this order
1. **Episode spine**: 1 thesis sentence, 3 claims the guest made, 1 number, 1 disagreement or surprise.
2. **Newsletter issue** (600–800 words, written business English): title that states the thesis; lede; three sections; "what I'd do with this" paragraph from Lan's seat as an operator; one link to the full video.
3. **YouTube**: title (under 60 characters, specific claim), 2-line description, 8–10 chapters with timestamps, 3 pinned-comment questions.
4. **Shorts / 视频号 clips**: 3–5 clip candidates with timestamp, hook line, on-screen caption (EN and ZH).
5. **LinkedIn post**: 120–180 words, first line is the claim, no hashtags beyond two, ends with a question.
6. **WeChat / 公众号 article**: ZH, 1,200–1,800 characters, subheads, a pull quote, ends with the Fragrant Hill introduction block.
7. **Sponsor recap** (if sponsored): 5 lines, deliverables shipped, links, next date.

## Style
- Lan's voice: operator, not journalist. Short sentences. Numbers in context. Dry humour allowed, never flattery of the guest.
- Translate meaning, not words. Chinese versions use industry terms as the industry uses them (具身智能, 结构光, 车规级).
- Always mark what is quoted vs. paraphrased.
