# Fragrant Hill · Encore sponsorship proposal

## What a sponsor is actually buying (rank in this order)
1. **Access and hospitality**: a room their clients, dealers and executives want to be in. For B2B brands (automotive, robotics, semiconductors) this is 60–70% of the value.
2. **Association**: the artist's name next to theirs in two markets.
3. **Content**: cut-down films, interviews and behind-the-scenes material they can use for 12 months.
4. **Media reach**: last, and only if the audience numbers are real.

## Package ladder (always three tiers, prices anchored high to low)
| Tier | Count | Includes |
|---|---|---|
| Presenting partner | 1 | Naming ("presented by"), stage and screen branding, 2 VIP tables per city, artist meet-and-greet, 1 private recital or client evening, content rights, first refusal next tour |
| Official partner | 2–3 | Category exclusivity, 1 VIP table per city, programme and screen branding, shared content |
| Supplier / hospitality | 4–6 | Tickets block, lounge branding, logo on materials |
Add a "+ activation" line: sponsors budget 1–2× the rights fee on activation; offer to produce it.

## Pricing logic
- Start from the tour budget the sponsorship must cover, add the Fragrant Hill margin (target 20–30% of gross sponsorship, or an agency commission of 15–20% if the tour is third-party produced).
- Cross-check against comparable classical-music and festival partnerships (see `framework/research/03-sponsorship-benchmarks.md`).
- Payment terms: 50% on signature, 50% 60 days before first show. This is what makes the two-month cash return possible.

## Document structure (one page)
1. Headline: the offer in one sentence with the artist, cities and dates.
2. Three numbers: audience in seats, cities, dates to decision.
3. Why this artist, why this audience, why now (3 short paragraphs).
4. Package ladder with prices.
5. Timeline with payment milestones.
6. What Fragrant Hill does (production, content, hospitality) and what the sponsor does (brand, activation, guest list).
7. Risks and how they are covered (artist cancellation insurance, venue confirmation, category exclusivity).

## Outreach email (written business English, under 120 words)
Subject: <Artist> European tour 2027 · presenting partner
First line names the mutual connection or the reason this brand. Second paragraph is the offer in two sentences. Third is one date option for a 20-minute call. No attachments on first contact; link the one-pager.
